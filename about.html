We make the things you use every day.

Glaze Lab started as a shared studio shelf and a stubborn question: why is a good mug so hard to find?

We throw each piece on the wheel, fire it twice and dip it in glazes mixed in small batches. Colours vary a little from piece to piece, and we think that is the point.

Everything we sell is food safe, sturdy enough for daily use and priced so you can fill a whole table, not just one shelf.

What makes us different

Glazes mixed in house

Our colours are tested and mixed in the studio, so every glaze is food safe and made to last.

Small batches
